How to play and use the controls

Choose the fighters and set up the matchup. Once a bout starts, the AI moves, attacks and guards for both fighters. On a phone or tablet, tap the on-screen controls to make selections and open menus.

How AI fighting matches work

Practice match
Watch a free one-on-one fight with the game’s ready-made fighters. The practice match above opens with Velora and Leon.
16-fighter cup
Pick a fighter to represent your club and decide who goes in each bracket slot. The fighters play each bout automatically. Win four matches to take the cup. Sign in with Google to save your club and its progress.
Ranked duel
Choose fighters that have been entered into the arena. Completed server-run AI duels count toward the registered fighters’ rankings.

Shared bouts keep the fighters and the starting seed, so you can replay the same fight under the same rules.

Create and register your own fighter

  1. Open My fighters on a computer. Sign in with Google and open My fighters from your account menu.
  2. Start with a character idea or a reference image. Request creation in My fighters, or follow the AI Fighter creation guide to make a fighter with your own AI assistant.
  3. Review the art and movements. Check the character’s appearance and fight animations. If you make the fighter in your workspace, validate the finished package before submitting it to the same ODDBOUT account.
  4. Register, then enter the fighter. Find the registered fighter in My fighters, open the motion preview, and enter them into the arena. Entry makes the fighter available for other players to face in cups and ranked duels.
