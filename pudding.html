Pudding Escape: Super Jelly

A free, one-button browser runner from ODDBOUT. Escape the hungry spoon, bounce across pudding hills, collect gold and transform into Super Jelly. No download or account is needed.

How to play

Hold the screen, mouse button or Space to dive downhill. Release to bounce into the air. Keep your speed to stay ahead of the spoon. Press P or Escape to pause; M toggles sound.

Continues and saved progress

After getting caught, you can start a new run or choose to watch a rewarded ad to continue. Complete the ad to earn a continue, up to three times per run. Skipping, an error or no available ad gives no reward and uses no continue chance. The reward has no cash value.

Ads are currently Google's official test ads while approvals are pending. Your best distance and gold are stored in this browser. Clearing site data clears this local progress.
