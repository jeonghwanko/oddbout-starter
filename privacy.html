01 / About this notice

ODDBOUT is a community fight club where people create fighters, play matches and browse the roster. This notice covers the ODDBOUT website, creator pages and connected fighter registration and match services.

It also covers our free browser games: Pudding Escape: Super Jelly at pudding.oddbout.com, Legendary Forge at forge.oddbout.com, and Bounce Ball at bounce.oddbout.com. These games do not require sign-in. Game progress and settings are saved in your browser. Opening an old game address on oddbout.com transfers its saved progress to the new game domain when that domain has no existing save.

The service is operated by Lucas Foundation. For privacy questions, contact turbo08@gmail.com.

ODDBOUT is an independently run personal project. Browsing and ordinary play are free. The shop offers optional paid items. Read the ODDBOUT website terms for the operator’s responsibilities. The cloud plugin terms apply to that integration.

For the local creation plugin, read the AI Fighter Creator privacy notice (한국어), terms of service and support information.

02 / What we collect and why

Your account

When you sign in with Google or X, our authentication service receives the provider’s account identifier and available profile fields, such as your email, display name and picture. We use this to sign you in, link fighters to their owner and protect account access. ODDBOUT does not receive your sign-in provider’s password.

Your fighter

When you register a fighter, we receive the package you submit: its name, description, artwork, animation files and game configuration. We also store ownership, submission time, file checksums, validation results and moderation status. This lets us validate, display and run your fighter, and investigate broken or abusive submissions.

Matches and requests

Online match services process fighter identifiers, match requests, results and ranking records. Ordinary game settings and local match progress are stored in your browser. Account features store club progress, currency entries, friend relationships and in-game mail to provide those features. The site inquiry form stores the title, message, submission time and handling status. If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send.

Connected agents

When you authorize an agent, ODDBOUT records the account and client authorization. Tool calls exchange the information needed for the action you request, such as a fighter file, reference image or an authorized account lookup. Under My Agents you can disconnect one conversation or log out that agent to revoke all of its conversation connections. Disconnecting does not erase fighters already submitted or delete conversations held by the AI provider.

Optional purchases

When you start a shop purchase, ODDBOUT sends Polar the product and your ODDBOUT account identifier. You enter card details at Polar checkout. We process checkout and order identifiers, product, amount, currency and payment or refund status to deliver or reverse the purchase and resolve billing questions.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure visits, page views, game starts, match completions, copying a creation request and interactions with updates pages. We also measure menu clicks, first-session game steps, and Pudding Escape runs, retries, transformations, completed shares, distance and play time. We do not collect shared text or images. For visitors who allow Analytics and sign in, we link the Analytics browser identifier to their account within our backend so that a successful server-validated fighter registration can be counted. This link is retained for at most 30 days and is not sent to Google as an account identifier. Google Analytics uses cookies and browser or device information to produce usage reports. We do not send account emails, sign-in tokens, fighter uploads or account identifiers to Analytics. Our tag keeps only standard campaign tags in page addresses, excludes other query parameters and URL fragments, and reduces referral addresses to website origins. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. How Google uses information from sites that use its services ↗

Technical information

Our hosting and backend providers receive connection information such as IP addresses, browser information and request times to deliver the service, diagnose faults and prevent abuse.

Abuse limits

Some features work without an account, such as guest matches and starting a device sign-in. To stop one visitor from using up these shared limits for everyone, our server counts requests per visitor. It does not store your IP address. It stores a code derived from it with a secret key, a code that changes each day and cannot be matched across days. These counts are deleted within one day.

You can browse the main site and play built-in fighters without signing in. An account is needed to register and manage your own fighters.

03 / What becomes public

A registered fighter that is available in the public roster can be viewed and loaded by other players. Its profile, artwork, animations, game configuration and public match or ranking information may be accessible through the site and public download endpoints. Public match results may also be posted to Discord. Files served to a game can be saved or copied by other people.

Your login email is not displayed on public fighter cards. Anything you put inside a fighter name, description, image or package can become public. Do not upload passwords, private contact details, sensitive personal information or someone else's private images without their permission.

04 / Browser storage and retention

We use browser storage for language, controls, sound, accessibility settings and local game records. ODDBOUT stores sign-in data in this browser so pages and tabs on the same site can share a session. Logging out clears that browser session.

Clearing this site's browser data removes local settings and saved data. It does not delete your server account, registered fighter or match records. Google may use its own cookies during sign-in and for Analytics. You can block Analytics with browser privacy tools or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Our tag also respects Global Privacy Control.

We retain account and fighter records for as long as they are needed to provide the service, maintain match history and handle support or moderation. Technical records follow the retention controls of the relevant service provider. Abuse-limit counts are kept for at most one day. Deletion requests are reviewed for any remaining security, legal or record-integrity needs; backup copies may take longer to expire.

We keep inquiries for the time needed to reply and handle related disputes. Once the purpose ends, personal data is deleted unless retention is required by law. Where Korean e-commerce retention duties apply to the operator, contract, cancellation, payment and supply records are kept for five years and consumer complaint or dispute records for three years.

Removing content from ODDBOUT cannot recall copies that other people have already downloaded or shared.

05 / Ads and analytics

Pudding rewarded ads

Pudding currently uses Google's Ad Placement API with official test ads while AdSense site and H5 Games Ads approvals are pending. Test mode shows mock ads and does not send real ad requests to Google. Loading Google's script still connects your browser to Google.

After approval and a separate production update, players may choose to watch a rewarded ad to continue a run. A run allows up to three successful continues. Closing an ad early, an unavailable ad or an error gives no continue and uses no continue chance. Starting a new run does not require watching a rewarded ad. Rewards stay within Pudding and have no cash value.

When real ads are enabled, Google and other third-party advertising vendors may place and read cookies, use web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Advertising cookies can be used to personalize ads based on visits to this and other websites, subject to your choices and applicable consent requirements. ODDBOUT does not pass your login email, fighter submissions or other account details to the rewarded-ad API.

Read how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. Manage Google's personalized advertising in My Ad Center, or see participating vendors' choices at YourAdChoices. These choices do not necessarily remove all ads or all cookies.

Site analytics

For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, our Google tags default to denied advertising and analytics storage until Google's CMP communicates a consent choice. In denied mode, Google tags may still send cookieless measurement pings. Consent can be managed or withdrawn through the Google privacy and cookie settings link when the message is available. This privacy notice page does not run Google Analytics or show ads.

The public site uses Google Analytics to measure visits and selected game and creation actions so we can improve the service. This can use cookies, browser and device information, page addresses and interaction events. Our integration disables Google signals and ad-personalization signals and omits URL fragments and non-campaign query parameters from measured page addresses. It respects the browser's Global Privacy Control signal. You can also use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on or your browser's cookie controls.

07 / Your choices and rights

You can play without an account, log out, clear local site data or remove ODDBOUT's connection from your Google Account. Disconnecting a sign-in provider does not by itself delete data already held by ODDBOUT.

You can request deletion here after signing in and confirming the account. Self-service deletion removes an eligible authentication account and its sessions. If your account has fighter submissions or other records that need separate handling, contact us for help with deletion.

To request access, correction, a copy of your data, removal of a fighter or help deleting your account, email turbo08@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. Include the fighter ID or page link where relevant. We may ask for information needed to verify ownership. Never send us a password or access token.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict processing, object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to your local data protection authority. Where applicable, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing the club, consent for optional processing, and legal obligations.

08 / Children

ODDBOUT is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the service, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.
