Animate your own 2D character and bring it into the ODDBOUT arena

Describe a character or bring a reference drawing to make walking, running, attacking and blocking animations. Download the animations you create for your own games or videos. Register the fighter package with ODDBOUT and, once it passes validation, take your fighter into a practice match.

Open ODDBOUT in ChatGPT

When you use ODDBOUT with your ChatGPT subscription, GPT work included in your plan incurs no separate OpenAI API charges. Available generation features and limits depend on your plan. Asking ODDBOUT to produce a fighter on its servers uses ODDBOUT creation tokens.

“Make me a raccoon blacksmith who fights in a scorched apron.”

  1. Describe your fighter

    Start with something you want to see on screen, like soot on the apron or the little grunt before a hammer swing. You can attach a sketch or reference image you have the right to use.

  2. Choose their appearance

    When ODDBOUT handles creation, you pick a front view. That design carries through walking, running, attacking, blocking, taking a hit and falling.

  3. Practise and enter the arena

    Open My fighters with the account you registered with. Try a practice bout and check all 11 required motions. Once the front image passes review and you enter the fighter, other players can meet it in cups and ranked duels. Control your fighter yourself or let the AI fight.

Find a game on ChatGPT web or in the app

On ChatGPT web and in the app, tell @ODDBOUT what you feel like playing. The launcher suggests two or three matches from 30 reviewed web games. Pick a card to play an ODDBOUT original in chat or an external game on its official Poki page.

On the plugin page, choose the option to try it in chat. In a new conversation, select @ODDBOUT and ask a question like the ones below.

One keyboard. Two very different contests.

We asked for two popular games that friends could play simultaneously on one keyboard. ODDBOUT returned Basketball Stars and Tag. In Basketball Stars, steal the ball and shoot before your friend can block you. In Tag, use separate keys to chase each other until you can pass the chase to your friend. Pick the kind of rivalry you feel like today.

Try a request that fits your setup

Playing on your phone

“Find two games I can play one-handed on my phone. A runner would be great.”

Phone and one-handed controls are requirements. A runner gets preference among the games that fit.

In the mood to build something

“I’d like to run a shop or merge materials. What would I do first?”

Compare the opening moves. Harvest bananas and stock a shelf in Monkey Mart, or combine ore and forge equipment in Legendary Forge.

Keep one condition and change another

“Keep the shared keyboard, but leave out the basketball game you just showed me.”

Keep your setup and rule out a previous pick. If only one game fits, you’ll see one.

Three ODDBOUT originals you can play in chat

Pudding Escape
Pick a jelly, run and dodge obstacles. Come back for another run to beat your score.
Legendary Forge
Tap a vein to mine ore, merge two matching pieces, then take them to the anvil to make equipment for delivery. The screenshot shows the forge after mining in a live game.

Choose the in-chat play button on an original to open the game inside the launcher. Return to the launcher to pick again. For an external game, choose the Poki button and confirm the link in ChatGPT to open the official game page.

Where the picks come from

The starting catalog pairs three ODDBOUT originals with 27 games selected from Poki’s popular list. The count on each external card is the cumulative number of Poki ratings when we reviewed it on November 21, 2026. Requirements such as simultaneous play or guest access narrow the shortlist first. Preferences such as genre help order the matches. If a requirement like ad-free play or an exact round length hasn’t been verified, ODDBOUT asks what you can relax.

정환 고 · 2026-11-06

01

What you can do

In ChatGPT, available tools can help you request a fighter, upload a reviewed package or profile set, practise, enter cups, manage a career and write in the workshop or worlds. Public stories and your chosen nickname are visible to others. Available tools depend on the app and its approved version.

02

Make it yours

Give ODDBOUT a description and up to two pictures you have permission to use. Choose an official worker, your own PC or an approved community host, then review the front view and motions. Official-worker use spends account tokens; sending your material to someone else’s PC requires your consent. You can also make and package files in your own workspace.

03

Connect from Dots

Open ODDBOUT in ChatGPT, choose Connect and authorize the account that owns your fighters.

04

Use it in Claude

Claude can use ODDBOUT too: read the game, spar, enter cups and tune your fighters. Connect ODDBOUT in Claude.

05

Register when ready

Register the package you reviewed and check the result. A validated fighter starts in production for owner preview and practice. To enter public rosters and other people’s competitions, review each required motion and enter the fighter with a front painting accepted by ODDBOUT.
