November 18, 2026UpdateRunning minigame added+

Run forever on ground that collapses behind you! Hold, spring and fly to beat your best.

Running Grand Prix

A standalone running web game alongside ODDBOUT. Behind you the ground falls away; ahead, hills rise and waves roll in. The game is currently in Korean.

One button

Hold to brace and wind up, let go to spring forward. Chain PERFECTs, BIG AIRs and sparks into combos and FEVER, and chase level goals and medals. No sign-in is required, and runs do not affect your ODDBOUT club record or rankings.
