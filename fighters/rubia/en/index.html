Character story

Always a beat early

Rubia used to slip in and out of sealed treasure vaults. She sizes strangers up from a distance, and when she thinks up a prank, the tip of her tail gives her away. She also reaches out to the audience first. Sometimes she starts the dance before they can react and has to catch Nera’s eye to find the beat again.

Rubia and Brika trade a line of song for a step. Rubia holds even her tail still to listen. During VICE’s encore she counts on her fingers where only Morne can see, and Morne answers by turning a page. For once, Rubia waits for the applause. What she wants now is a light stick with her own name on it.

“Sure, I'm fast. That doesn't mean I'm ahead of the beat.”
