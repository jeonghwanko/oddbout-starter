Character story

The night the applause wouldn’t stop

Velora once brought rivals to heel with contracts and decrees. She liked to decide where every conversation began and ended. The Demon Lord’s castle tour seemed to her like another ceremony for teaching obedience. When the applause lasted beyond the final note, she called it a refusal to follow the closing order.

A steady low vocal opens the song, and a small gesture brings the other members in together. When Nera called for a break, Velora heard a challenge to her authority. Only later did she realize that Nera had noticed her voice going hoarse. After that first encore, Velora began preparing an extra song in secret.

“This time it is not an order. Will you listen to the end?”

Awakening

Crimson Moon Gale

A red moon rises behind Velora. She drives her opponent back with crescent-shaped blasts, lifts them in a crimson whirlwind, and brings them crashing down.
