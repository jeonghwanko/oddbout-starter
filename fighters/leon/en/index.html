Character story

A song to pay for a night at the inn

Leon grew up in a farming village. When someone needs a hand, he jumps in before he’s heard the whole plan. He’s used to putting himself between danger and his friends and has a harder time asking them for help. The four adventurers once sang in an inn to pay for their lodging. Word of the song reached the next village, which asked them to perform.

When he’s nervous, Leon grips the edge of his cape and lets it go. As soon as it’s time to sing, he looks someone in the audience in the eye and gives them the first line. At a joint rehearsal, he enters a beat early and Nera stops the music. During the show, he finds his way back with the audience’s clapping and Nera shifts a step to match him.

“I’ll lead the way. Could Noel handle the introductions?”

Awakening

Final Verse Slash

Two spotlights come on, and Leon marks each beat with a blue slash. He sends his opponent into the air for crossing cuts. On the last line, a giant sword falls from above the stage.
