Character story

The beat a teammate missed

Nera crossed the rift of 1977 into Yoonion. She believes that if she loses her footing, the whole line will follow. She runs the strictest rehearsals and is the last to leave a teammate who has slipped up. When Rubia comes in a beat early, Nera catches her eye to bring her back in time. Later, she asks about Rubia’s ankle as though it were a routine training check.

The feelings Nera keeps out of her clipped replies come through when she carries the melody. She stops a joint rehearsal when Leon comes in early. At the show, she sees him keep singing to the audience’s clapping. She listens to the applause too, then takes a step to match his movement.

“That beat just now… I'll match it. Keep going.”

Awakening

Downbeat

Three teal conducting strokes draw the opponent in to the metronome. After a moment of silence, the fourth beat sends them upward. A closing stroke brings them down.
