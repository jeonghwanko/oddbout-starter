Character story

From a choir of the dead to a living stage

Morne hears each of the scattered voices of the dead and brings them together as a choir. She has worked with skeletons that follow a cue for so long that she tends to skip the explanation. At VICE’s first rehearsal, she finished the arrangement and forgot to hand out the sheet music. Nobody sang a note.

Her clipped, low rap keeps the beat beneath Velora’s sustained bass lines. She usually has one eye on her spellbook, but she opens both eyes as soon as the music starts. She remembers where each member can sing comfortably and where they need to take a breath.

“A living choir asks… a lot of questions.”

Awakening

Dream Lullaby

Violet mist rolls in as Morne hums, and notes settle over her opponent one beat at a time. When they start to nod off, she launches them and brings a curtain of dreams down on them.

VICE

Four witches from the Demon Lord’s castle

Velora, Nera, Rubia and Morne crossed from the Demon Lord’s castle into Yoonion. On stage they perform as the idol group VICE. In the ring, they fight as the Nightcrown Fighters Club.
